Privacy

The short version: what you enter, where it goes and who can see it.

Reviewing on your own

  • Your answers are kept on your device (in your browser) so you can come back to them.
  • Nothing is sent anywhere unless you share your report card or invite people to rate you.

Crews and invite links

  • When you join a crew or invite people, reviews are stored in Google Firebase so the crew can see results.
  • Crew members see combined crew scores only, and only once at least 2 people have reviewed someone. Members never see who gave which score.
  • The crew admin (the person who set up the crew) can see each review, including who wrote it, so they can manage the crew.
  • The crew admin can delete reviews, reset people or reset the whole crew.

Sharing your report card

  • The report card picture is made on your device and sent through your phone's own share menu.
  • We keep a simple count of shares (how it was shared and the grade), not who you sent it to.

Signing in

  • Only crew admins sign in, with Google. We store your name and email so you can manage your crew.
  • Everyone else joins with an invite link and doesn't need an account.

What we don't do

  • We don't sell your data or show ads.
  • We don't ask for phone numbers or contact lists.

Removing your data

  • Your own answers: use Start again on the review page, or clear this site's data in your browser.
  • Crew reviews: ask your crew admin to delete them, or contact the site owner through ratemyyear.com.
